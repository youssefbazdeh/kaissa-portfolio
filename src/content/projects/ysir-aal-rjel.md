  - type: context
    label: "The saying"
    text: >-
      In Tunisia, the expression "Ysir al rjel" (literally: it could happen to men) is often used
      to ease a situation, meaning that a man can overcome hardships. It downplays the seriousness
      of an incident while affirming a man's strength, masculinity and resilience. Deeply rooted in
      Tunisian culture, it also conveys solidarity and support toward someone facing a challenge.

  - type: context
    label: "Why men"
    text: >-
      A man can be directly or indirectly affected by this disease: although he may be at risk himself,
      he also has an essential role to play with the women in his life, whether it's his wife, sister,
      mother or even colleagues. His support can be crucial in encouraging those around him to get
      screened and in breaking the silence around the illness.
  - type: execution-list
